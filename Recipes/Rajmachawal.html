Rajma Chawal Recipe

Back to Home

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rajma (kidney beans)
  • 2 medium onions
  • 2–3 medium tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1–2 green chillies
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp rajma masala (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Water as needed

Steps To Make Delicious Rajma Chawal

  1. Soak the rajma in water overnight or for at least 8 hours.
  2. Drain the soaked rajma and pressure cook it with enough water and a pinch of salt until soft (about 15–20 minutes).
  3. In a pan, heat oil or ghee. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  4. Add finely chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chillies. Sauté for a minute.
  6. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they turn soft and mushy.
  7. Add turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, and salt. Mix well.
  8. Add the cooked rajma along with some of its cooking water. Simmer for 10–15 minutes on low heat.
  9. Sprinkle garam masala and rajma masala (if using). Mix well and cook for another 5 minutes.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
  11. Serve hot with steamed rice (chawal).
