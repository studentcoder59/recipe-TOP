Chole Bhature Recipe

Back to Home

Ingredients To Make Chole Bhature:

  • 1 cup dry kabuli chana (chickpeas)
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp chole masala
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish
  • Optional: tea bag for darker-coloured chole, bay leaf and amchur

Steps To Make Delicious Chole Bhature

  1. Soak the dry kabuli chana in water for 2-3 hours.
  2. Pressure cook the chana with salt and water until soft.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  4. Add chopped onions and cook until golden brown.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chillies.
  6. Include tomato puree and cook for 5 minutes.
  7. Add all the spices and cook for another 2 minutes.
  8. Stir in the cooked chana and mix well.
  9. Simmer for 10-15 minutes on low heat.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with bhature.
