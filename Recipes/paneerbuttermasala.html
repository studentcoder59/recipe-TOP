Paneer Butter Masala Recipe

Back to Home

Ingredients:

  • 200g paneer, cubed
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  2. Add chopped onions and cook until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Include tomato puree and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add all the spices and cook for another 2 minutes.
  6. Stir in the heavy cream and bring to a boil.
  7. Add the cubed paneer and simmer for 10 minutes.
  8. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with naan or rice.
